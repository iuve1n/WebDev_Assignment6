Accordion

Select a question to open its answer. Only one answer stays open at a time.

jQuery is a JavaScript library that makes common page tasks, such as selecting elements and handling clicks, shorter to write.

An accordion shows or hides a section of content when its button is selected.

The click handler uses jQuery slide methods to show the selected answer and close the others.
